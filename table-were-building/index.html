The Table We’re Building

A Liberating Scripture Collective podcast

Hosted by Brandon Vélez Johnson and Aaron J. Smith

The Table We’re Building is a conversation with a trauma therapist and a spiritual director––wounded healers reclaiming faith after abuse, naming harm without shame, and crafting a bigger, braver table for belonging.

Pull up a chair for candid stories from fellow survivors, therapists, theologians, and activists who refuse to surrender‌ Jesus and his work to the forces that weaponized them. Together we’ll practice consent-based spirituality, celebrate chosen family, and imagine a faith wide enough for everybody.
