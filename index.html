The Liberating Scripture Collective

The Liberating Scripture Collective is a growing constellation of people devoted to reading the Christian scriptures in ways that tell the truth about power, trauma, liberation, and human dignity. It brings together Bible translation, teaching, conversation, and spiritual accompaniment, shaped by the conviction that scripture should not be used to control, exclude, or harm, but to restore, free, and gather. We’re coming together as people committed to liberating Scripture from systems of domination, exclusion, and harm, uncovering the ways Scripture liberates us in turn.

One central expression of this work is the Liberation and Inclusion Translation (LIT) of the New Testament, a justice-oriented, trauma-informed translation project grounded in careful study and theological transparency. The translation itself lives at litbible.net, where the text, notes, and ongoing work are freely available.

This site is a place where related work is beginning to take shape, including public conversation through The Table We’re Building podcast and one-to-one spiritual direction grounded in presence, attentiveness, and care. The Collective itself is still becoming. This space is being built slowly, with intention. We invite you to join us.
